# Example: AI-native wealth advice for mass-affluent US households

A complete report produced with this skill's method, as of 15 November 2026.

- [report.pdf](report.pdf): the paper, 12 pages
- [report.md](report.md): the same in Markdown
- [evidence.csv](evidence.csv): 112 sourced facts from 44 sources and 15 labelled estimates

**The idea:** an AI-native financial adviser for US households with roughly $100,000 to $1 million to invest, in two variants: the AI leads, or a human adviser leads with AI in the background.

**The verdict:** no-go for both consumer variants. The report says why on page one.

## What to look at

- **Page one** gives the verdict, the five facts behind it and the scorecard.
- **Section 7** admits that only one company in the market discloses both revenue and valuation, so the multiple rests on a single data point.
- **Section 11** lists the searches run against each claimed gap.
- **Section 16** contains a correction: a gap claimed early in the run was withdrawn after reading the provider's own page. It also lists four conflicts between sources.

## What was left out

The original run also used private data supplied by the founder. It is omitted from this public version, and the report says so where it would appear.

## Read this before quoting it

Figures are as reported by the cited sources on the access date. Company claims are marked as claims. Private companies disclose little, and the report writes "n/d" where a figure was not found. This is research support for evaluating a startup idea. It is not investment advice, and it is not a recommendation for or against any company named in it.
